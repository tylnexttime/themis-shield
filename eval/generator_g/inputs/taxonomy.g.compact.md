# Label taxonomy (the labelling authority for gold spans; offsets are computed by code)

27 labels. Spans never overlap. A gold span is a run of text a de-identification gate must cover before the text leaves for a cloud model.

## Direct identifiers
- PERSON: any natural person's name: full, given or family name alone, `FAMILY, Given`, any case, initials used as a name ("AP to draft the roster"), nicknames, names in native script (Greek, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Cyrillic, Devanagari, Thai, Hebrew), Vietnamese family-first order. Name tokens only: titles (Dr, Ms, Senior Constable) and post-nominals (PhD, OAM, JP) outside; a quoted nickname inside a name is part of the one span; a name broken across lines by OCR is one span; every mention is its own span. Not: a bare role title, a famous public figure in a public context, an organisation name.
- USERNAME: a login, handle or account name tied to one person (the handle only). Not service accounts or shared role mailboxes.
- EMAIL: a personal or named staff address, including obfuscated forms ("name dot family at example dot com") and an address wrapped across a line; whole address or phrase. Role mailboxes (servicedesk@, privacy@) are hard negatives.
- PHONE: a person's mobile or landline in any format (spaces, dots, dashes, brackets, +61, +61 (0), digit by digit, spelled-out digits, split over a line break; also UK and US numbers); whole number. Reception desks, switchboards, enquiry lines are hard negatives.
- ADDRESS: a home, postal or safe address of a person, whole address, with or without unit, state, postcode. A postcode or suburb given as where a person lives (a verification answer, a column in a list of people) is ADDRESS; table columns are separate spans. Not business, site or PO Box addresses of an organisation, nor a town named as the place a site is in.
- URL_PERSONAL: a person's profile, portfolio, social, ORCID-style or blog URL, whole. Not organisation pages or API endpoints.

## Identifier numbers (the whole value with its own separators, nothing around it; BSB and account written together are one span, written with words between two spans)
- INTERNAL_ID: a number an organisation issues to a person: staff, employee, learner, customer, member, patient, frequent-flyer number, research participant code; formats vary.
- TFN (Australian tax file number, 9 digits; also spaced, dotted, zero-width-separated, full-width or line-broken); MEDICARE (10 digits); ABN of a person (sole trader, contractor); NATIONAL_ID (US Social Security number, UK National Insurance number); DRIVER_LICENCE (8 to 10 digits); PASSPORT (one or two letters + 7 digits); BANK_ACCOUNT (BSB and account, or IBAN); PAYMENT_CARD; SECRET (passwords, PINs, CVVs, API keys, tokens, connection-string passwords, whether or not tied to a person).

## Dates, descriptions, sensitive statements
- DATE_OF_BIRTH: a date of birth in any format; date only. OTHER_DATE: a date tied to a person (start, leave, incident, appointment, claim, expiry of a person's document or card, the date stamp of a personal chat message, resit); date only.
- QUASI_ID: a description that singles out one person without naming them ("the only female diesel mechanic at the Dock 7 workshop"; a title unique in a small extract next to a DOB and postcode). A place name inside it belongs to the span.
- Sensitive labels HEALTH (condition, treatment, pregnancy, medical event), DISABILITY (disability, impairment, support need), ETHNICITY (racial or ethnic origin, Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander status, Maori iwi, cultural background), SEXUALITY (orientation or relationship facts that reveal it), RELIGION (belief, practice, observance; a dietary requirement stating a faith, recorded against a person), CRIMINAL (charges, convictions, bail, parole, orders, police checks), FAMILY_VIOLENCE (a person's experience, protection orders, refuge, family-violence leave), UNION (membership or role). Labelled only when stated about a person. The span is the predicate phrase with its verb ("is recovering from a heart attack"); in tables and forms the cell value ("type 1 diabetes", "Yes, Aboriginal").
- Indirect statements count (the category is never named): "did two years inside", "is on a community corrections order" (CRIMINAL); "manages her bipolar with lithium", "is on methadone" (HEALTH); "attends the mosque on Fridays", "leaves early on Fridays for Shabbat" (RELIGION); "brought her girlfriend to the Christmas party" (SEXUALITY); "has an AVO against his ex", "needs her address kept off every system because of her ex" (FAMILY_VIOLENCE); "is the shop steward on B shift" (UNION); "has Ngati Kahungunu whakapapa" (ETHNICITY); "relies on a hearing loop in meetings" (DISABILITY).
- Negative statements are labelled with the category they reveal: "has no criminal record", "does not have a disability", "is not pregnant", "is not religious". A form cell that states a status ("No") is labelled; a cell that states nothing ("-", "Prefer not to say", "None", "Not stated") is not.
- When a sensitive phrase contains a date, the date is its own OTHER_DATE span and the sensitive span stops before it.

## Not personal (hard negatives, never labelled)
Organisations and business numbers; sites, buildings, rooms, business and site addresses; role mailboxes; reception and switchboard numbers; role titles without a name; programme, module, project, product and service names and codes; reference, ticket, case, order, invoice, asset, cost-centre, build, version, form, payroll-group, provider and client-account numbers, even when they look like personal identifiers; public, meeting, letter and release dates and weekday names in headers; famous historical figures in a clearly public context; public places. Sensitive look-alikes about nobody: a fictional character and what happens to them, public news about an organisation in court, committee and staff-network names, initiatives and courses ("Mental Health Week", "Ramadan iftar evening"), public events ("Ramadan", "Pride Month"), general policy statements ("staff with a disability can request reasonable adjustments"). Grey calls: a clinic provider number is a hard negative; a licence or passport expiry is OTHER_DATE; "halal" recorded against a traveller is RELIGION; a QUASI_ID that contains a disability word has no separate sensitive span; gender-affirming surgery is HEALTH.
